
# E-Commerce Marketplace Marketing Analytics | Customer Behavior, Revenue & Business Performance Analysis

##  Project Overview

In today's competitive e-commerce industry, understanding customer behavior, monitoring business performance, and identifying revenue opportunities are essential for sustainable business growth.

This project focuses on **Marketing Analytics for an E-Commerce Marketplace**, with the objective of analyzing business performance and generating actionable insights from customer, seller, product, order, payment, and review data.

The analysis explores various aspects of an e-commerce business, including revenue trends, customer acquisition, customer retention, purchasing patterns, seller performance, product popularity, payment preferences, and customer satisfaction.

Using data analytics techniques, this project aims to identify meaningful business patterns, understand customer and seller behavior, discover cross-selling opportunities, and support data-driven marketing and business decisions.

**Project Domain:** E-Commerce | Marketing Analytics | Business Analytics

**Dataset Period:** September 2016 – October 2018

---

##  Business Problem

An online e-commerce marketplace wants to measure, manage, and analyze its overall business performance.

The business aims to understand:

- Who are the most valuable customers?
- Which sellers contribute the highest revenue?
- Which products and categories generate the most sales?
- How many new customers are acquired every month?
- Are existing customers returning to make repeat purchases?
- What are the monthly revenue and sales trends?
- Which products are frequently purchased together?
- Which payment methods are most preferred by customers?
- How satisfied are customers with different products and categories?

The goal is to convert raw transactional data into meaningful insights that can help the business improve marketing strategies, customer relationships, revenue generation, and overall operational performance.

---

##  Project Objectives

The major objectives of this project are:

1. Perform detailed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to understand overall business performance.
2. Analyze customer acquisition, retention, and purchasing behavior.
3. Identify revenue trends and seasonal sales patterns.
4. Segment customers and sellers based on their revenue contribution.
5. Discover frequently purchased product combinations through Cross-Selling Analysis.
6. Analyze customer payment preferences and payment channel usage.
7. Evaluate customer satisfaction through product and category ratings.
8. Generate actionable business insights to support strategic decision-making.

##  Project Analysis & Methodology

### 1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Performed detailed exploratory analysis to understand the structure, quality, distribution, and characteristics of the data.

**Key Analysis:**
- Total Revenue and Total Quantity Sold.
- Total Customers, Products, Categories, and Sellers.
- Total Business Locations.
- Total Payment Methods.
- Revenue and Sales Performance.
- Category-wise and Location-wise Revenue.
- Monthly, Weekly, and Daily Sales Trends.
- Top 10 Most Expensive Products.
- Popular Products by Month, Seller, State, and Category.

### 2. Customer Acquisition & Retention Analysis

Analyzed customer purchasing patterns to understand how effectively the business acquires new customers and retains existing customers.

**Key Analysis:**
- Monthly New Customer Acquisition.
- Month-on-Month Customer Retention.
- New vs Existing Customer Revenue.
- Repeat Purchase Behavior.
- Customer Retention Trends.
- Cohort Analysis to understand customer purchasing activity over time.

### 3. Revenue & Sales Trend Analysis

Analyzed revenue and sales performance across different business dimensions to identify growth opportunities and seasonal patterns.

**Key Analysis:**
- Monthly Revenue Trends.
- Category-wise Revenue Contribution.
- State-wise and Location-wise Sales.
- Quantity Sold by Category.
- Product Performance by Month.
- Weekly and Daily Sales Patterns.
- Identification of High-Performing and Low-Performing Categories.

### 4. Customer & Seller Segmentation

Segmented customers and sellers based on the revenue they generate to identify valuable business relationships and understand revenue contribution.

**Key Analysis:**
- Customer Segmentation Based on Revenue.
- Seller Segmentation Based on Revenue.
- Identification of High-Value Customers.
- Identification of Top Revenue-Generating Sellers.
- Revenue Contribution Across Different Segments.

### 5. Cross-Selling Analysis

Performed cross-selling analysis to identify products that customers frequently purchase together in the same transaction.

**Key Analysis:**
- Identify Frequently Purchased Product Combinations.
- Analyze Two-Product and Three-Product Combinations.
- Find the Top 10 Product Combinations.
- Discover Opportunities for Product Bundling.
- Support Product Recommendation and Cross-Selling Strategies.

### 6. Payment Behavior Analysis

Analyzed customer payment patterns to understand how customers prefer to pay for their purchases.

**Key Analysis:**
- Distribution of Different Payment Methods.
- Most Frequently Used Payment Channels.
- Customer Payment Preferences.
- Payment Method Usage Patterns.
- Payment Behavior Across Transactions.

### 7. Customer Satisfaction & Review Analysis

Evaluated customer satisfaction using product and category ratings to identify areas of strong and weak customer experience.

**Key Analysis:**
- Top 10 Highest-Rated Product Categories.
- Top 10 Lowest-Rated Product Categories.
- Top 10 Highest-Rated Products.
- Top 10 Lowest-Rated Products.
- Average Ratings by Location.
- Average Ratings by Seller.
- Average Ratings by Product and Category.
- Monthly Customer Satisfaction Trends.
